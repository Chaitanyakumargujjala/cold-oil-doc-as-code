## 2. What we're building

A cold-pressed / wood-pressed edible oil business that sells directly to customers across India, where every bottle carries a QR code linking to a page that tells the customer exactly which farm, which variety, and which press date produced their oil.

**Long-term value chain (vision):**
```
Own groundnut farm → seed grading → oil extraction → bottling → 2B website → customer
```

**Initial test model (Stage 1–2):**
```
Sourced seeds → pressed at partner facility → bottled → 2B website → customer (Hyderabad)
```

The initial test does not require owning the full chain. It needs to validate demand, price, packaging, and repeat purchase before committing capital.

## 4. Customer

Long-term target: any Indian household buying cooking oil.  
Initial test target: health-conscious, urban households in Hyderabad willing to pay a premium for traceable, quality groundnut oil — likely already buying premium grocery online.

Initial geography: **Hyderabad** (founder-based, first 50–100 orders).  
Scale to all-India after delivery economics and packaging safety are proven.

---

## 5. Sales model

**Stage 1–2 (now):** D2C website only
- Guest checkout
- Online payment + COD
- Hyderabad-first delivery, then nationwide
- No standard returns — Contact Us for damage or leakage

**Stage 3+ (only after pilot proves demand):**
- Website subscriptions
- Quick-commerce apps (Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart)
- Possibly retail or B2B

---

## 6. Trust and differentiation

2B earns trust through:
- Named groundnut variety and origin on every bottle
- Plain explanation of the pressing process
- Batch-level QR code → customer page (variety, press date, press location)
- Lab test results when available
- No jargon, no unverifiable claims

**Rule:** Never claim cold-pressed, organic, variety-specific, or origin-specific without documented supplier evidence.

## 8. First milestone — 10kg home test

**Assumption:** 10kg of seeds (Java, Bold, Tac) pressed at Sneha Ganuga Oils and Foods, Hyderabad.

**Cost estimate (all assumptions — get real quotes):**
- Seeds: ~₹5,000 for 10kg total across 3 varieties
- Extraction: ~₹600
- Total: ~₹5,600 — well under ₹10,000

**Expected yield:** ~2–2.5 litres per 10kg of whole seeds, cold pressed — confirm exact yield with Sneha Ganuga.

**What to evaluate during home test:**

| Parameter | What to observe |
|-----------|----------------|
| Colour | Light vs dark — note difference across varieties |
| Raw aroma | Smell before heating |
| Cooked aroma | Smell when heated in pan |
| Taste | Neutral tasters preferred; note distinct character |
| Smoke point | At what temperature does each variety start smoking? |
| Cooking performance | Does it behave well for tempering, frying, sautéing? |
| Yield | Litres of oil from 10kg of seeds — ask and record |
| Seed price per kg | Get actual quote from supplier |
| Extraction cost | Get actual quote from supplier |
