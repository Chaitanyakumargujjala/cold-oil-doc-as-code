### FSSAI guidance note

FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) registration is **mandatory before selling any packaged food in India**, including edible oil.

There are two tiers relevant to a small D2C business:

| Type | Annual turnover | Fee | Validity |
|------|----------------|-----|----------|
| Basic Registration | Below ₹12 lakh/year | ₹100/year | 1–5 years |
| State License | ₹12 lakh – ₹20 crore/year | ₹2,000–₹5,000/year | 1–5 years |

**For the pilot test phase:** Basic Registration is sufficient if total sales stay below ₹12 lakh/year. Apply at [foscos.fssai.gov.in](https://foscos.fssai.gov.in).

**What you need to apply:**
- Aadhaar / PAN of proprietor
- Address proof for the business premises
- Nature of business (food manufacturer / repacker / seller)
- List of food products (groundnut oil, cold-pressed)

**Important:** The FSSAI number must appear on your product label before you sell a single bottle. This is non-negotiable.

**Immediate action:** Go to [foscos.fssai.gov.in](https://foscos.fssai.gov.in), start a Basic Registration application. It typically takes 7–30 days.
